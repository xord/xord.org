# Midified

## Privacy Policy

Midified does not collect information about you. It does not connect to the internet, and sends no information out of your Mac.

**What is kept on your Mac**

Midified keeps your config, which maps the notes and the controllers of your MIDI devices to the keys, and where the MIDI Monitor was on the screen, on your Mac, in the app's own data folder.

**MIDI Events**

Midified receives the MIDI events of your devices only to press the keys your config maps them to. The MIDI Monitor shows the events and the keys sent while the app runs, and they are not saved.

**The Permission of Accessibility**

Midified asks for the permission of Accessibility only to send the keys your config maps the MIDI events to. It does not read what other apps show, nor what you type on your keyboard.

**Crash Reports**

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in the settings of macOS, Apple may send crash reports of the app, by its own policy, which do not identify you.

**Children's Privacy**

Midified does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

**Changes to This Privacy Policy**

This policy may be updated from time to time, as the app changes, and the changes will be posted on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-11-20.

**Contact Us**

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at [support@xord.org](mailto:support@xord.org).
