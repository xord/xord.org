title: Desktop Golfer 2D Privacy Policy
---

**Privacy Policy**

Desktop Golfer 2D does not ask for information that identifies you directly, such as your name or your email address. It collects how it is used, as described below.

**What is kept on your Mac**

The game keeps the record of your play, such as your shots, the holes you have played and the achievements you have earned, and your settings, such as the sound and the language, on your Mac, in the game's own data folder.

**Usage Analytics**

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC, to see how the game is used and to make it better.

Through the service, the game collects when it is opened and how long it is used, how it is played, such as the shots and the holes finished, the version of the game, information about the OS and the device, the rough region you are in, such as the country, and an identifier of the installation of the game.

The information is used to see the number of players each day, how they keep playing and how each feature is used, and not to show or personalize advertisements. No information that identifies you directly, such as your name or your email address, is sent as the data.

The information is sent to Google, and handled under its terms. For the details, see:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**Crash Reports**

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in the settings of macOS, Apple may send crash reports of the game, by its own policy, which do not identify you.

**Children's Privacy**

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**Changes to This Privacy Policy**

This policy may be updated from time to time, as the game changes, and the changes will be posted on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-11-13.

**Contact Us**

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at [support@xord.org](mailto:support@xord.org).
